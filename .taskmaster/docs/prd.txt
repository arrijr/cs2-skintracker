# CS2 Skin Tracker - Phase 5: Advanced Automation & Social Features

## Product Overview
The CS2 Skin Tracker is a comprehensive web application for monitoring and analyzing CS2 skin prices with advanced portfolio management, market intelligence, and social features. Phase 5 focuses on advanced automation, social trading features, and institutional-grade tools.

## Current Status
- Phase 4 Complete: Portfolio Health Score + Market Intelligence
- 25,959 CS2 skins imported from Steam Web API
- 140,110+ price history entries for analytics
- Full authentication via Clerk (OAuth + email/password)
- Complete portfolio management with P/L tracking
- Advanced market analysis and health scoring

## Phase 5 Objectives

### 1. Advanced Portfolio Automation
- **Portfolio Rebalancing**: Automated rebalancing recommendations based on risk tolerance and market conditions
- **Tax Loss Harvesting**: Smart selling strategies to optimize tax efficiency
- **Risk Management Rules**: Dynamic position sizing based on volatility and market conditions
- **Performance Optimization**: AI-driven suggestions for portfolio improvement

### 2. Social Trading Features
- **Community Insights**: Share portfolio strategies and performance with the community
- **Social Trading**: Follow successful traders and copy their strategies
- **Market Sentiment**: Community-driven analysis and sentiment tracking
- **Collaborative Tools**: Team portfolio management and shared watchlists

### 3. Institutional Features
- **Advanced Reporting**: Professional-grade analytics and reporting tools
- **Compliance Tools**: Regulatory reporting and audit trails
- **Multi-Portfolio Management**: Institutional account management with multiple portfolios
- **API Access**: Third-party integrations and developer access

### 4. Enhanced Market Intelligence
- **Real News API Integration**: Replace mock data with actual CS2 market news
- **Advanced ML Models**: Improve prediction accuracy with machine learning
- **Custom Thresholds**: User-defined health score parameters and alerts
- **Mobile Optimization**: Enhanced responsive design for mobile devices
